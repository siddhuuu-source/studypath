Privacy Policy

StudyPath by Siddhant. Last updated 21 November 2026.

What StudyPath is

StudyPath is a free learning and career guidance website and app for students. It lives at siddhuuu-source.github.io/studypath.

What we collect

  • Sign-in details. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google through Firebase Authentication. We use them to create your account and show your name in the app.
  • Questions, photos and chat messages. When AI features are available, the text you type and any photo you take of a question are sent to an AI service so it can write an answer. Do not include personal details such as your address or phone number in questions or photos.
  • Basic technical data. Our hosting and sign-in providers may log things like your IP address and browser type to keep the service working and secure.

How we use it

We use your information only to let you sign in, to answer your questions, and to run and improve the service. We do not sell your personal information and we do not show advertising.

Who else handles your data

Your sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. AI answers are generated by an AI provider when that feature is turned on. These services have their own privacy policies.

Your choices

  • You can log out at any time using the Log out button.
  • To ask us to delete your account or any data we hold about you, email us at the address below.

Students and children

StudyPath is made for students. If you are under 13, please use it together with a parent or guardian.

Accuracy of answers

Answers, practice questions and career suggestions are generated by AI and can contain mistakes. Check important information with your teacher, textbook or official sources. This is not professional career, medical or legal advice.
